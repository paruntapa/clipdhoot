# Bundled assets

- `face_landmarker.task` is Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker model, redistributed under Apache License 2.0. The license is included in `MEDIAPIPE_APACHE_LICENSE.txt`.
- `yunet.onnx` is the OpenCV Zoo YuNet face detector, redistributed under the MIT license. The license is included in `OPENCV_ZOO_MIT_LICENSE.txt`.
- `fonts/Montserrat-ExtraBold.ttf` is Montserrat, redistributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full license is in `fonts/OFL.txt`.
